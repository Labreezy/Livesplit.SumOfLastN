# Sum of Last N Splits
### AKA "did i pb anywhere.dll"

This is a plugin that keeps track of the best stretch of N splits over a period of time, designed for SA2 hunting categories.  If you PB on splits 4-24, the verifier is no longer forced to retime the menu after split 3 with this plugin in the mix.

This way, you can do (stage)x50s or what have you and take a look at your best time over that total session.

# Instructions

* Place the dll in the releases section in your livesplit's `Components` folder.
* Make sure your splits have menuing subsections for any SA2 hunting category. If they don't, you'll have to retime the menu into the first set out of your displayed time, which is possible but annoying.
* Add the component to your layout (it's under "Information")
* In the settings, you can set either 39 (20 sets, 19 menus between) total or 49 (25 sets, 24 menus between) for Knuckles or Rouge, respectively. (or any number, these are the two that make the most sense)

Happy hunting!
